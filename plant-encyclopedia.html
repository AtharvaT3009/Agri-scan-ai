Plant Encyclopedia

Explore a starter collection of familiar plants, their characteristics and basic growing needs.

🌿 Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Type: Aromatic herb

Known for its fragrant leaves and use in traditional home gardens.

Care: Bright sunlight, well-draining soil and moderate watering.

🌻 Sunflower

Type: Flowering plant

Recognised for its large flower head and seeds.

Care: Plenty of sunlight, space to grow and regular watering while establishing.

🍅 Tomato

Type: Fruit-bearing vegetable crop

Tomato plants produce edible fruits and are commonly grown in kitchen gardens.

Care: Full sun, support for many varieties and consistent soil moisture.

🌵 Aloe Vera

Type: Succulent

Stores water in its thick, fleshy leaves.

Care: Bright light, gritty well-draining soil and infrequent watering.

🥭 Mango

Type: Tropical fruit tree

A long-lived tree that produces the popular mango fruit in warm climates.

Care: Warm conditions, plenty of sunlight and room for roots and branches.

🌱 Mint

Type: Aromatic herb

Grows quickly and is known for its fresh-smelling leaves.

Care: Moist but well-drained soil and partial to full sunlight. It can spread quickly, so a pot helps contain it.

Plant-care needs vary with climate, season, soil and variety. These are general starting points, not fixed rules.
