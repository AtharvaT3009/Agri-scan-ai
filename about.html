About AGRI-SCAN AI

About the project

AGRI-SCAN AI is an educational project that explores how artificial intelligence and image classification can support plant-health awareness. A user uploads a leaf photograph, and a trained image model predicts which category the leaf most closely matches.

The website also brings together a disease library, a plant encyclopedia and simple plant-care guidance in one place.
