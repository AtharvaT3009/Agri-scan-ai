Plant Disease Library

A quick introduction to some common plant health problems. Similar symptoms can have different causes, so check the whole plant and its growing conditions.

FUNGAL DISEASE

Leaf Spot

Small brown, black or yellow spots may appear on leaves. Some leaf-spot diseases spread more easily when foliage stays wet.

General prevention

Avoid overcrowding, water near the soil and remove badly affected fallen leaves.

FUNGAL DISEASE

Powdery Mildew

A white, powder-like coating may develop on the surface of leaves and stems.

General prevention

Give plants enough space and airflow, and avoid excess shade where possible.

WATER-RELATED

Root Rot

Roots can become damaged when soil remains waterlogged for too long. The plant may look weak or wilted.

General prevention

Use well-draining soil and containers with drainage holes. Water according to the plant's needs.

PESTS

Aphids

Tiny insects may gather on new growth and the undersides of leaves. Leaves can curl or become sticky.

General prevention

Inspect new growth regularly and encourage a healthy garden ecosystem.

BACTERIAL DISEASE

Bacterial Leaf Blight

Some bacterial diseases cause water-soaked-looking areas, yellowing or browning. Symptoms vary by plant.

General prevention

Use clean gardening tools and avoid handling plants when they are wet.

GENERAL STRESS

Nutrient Deficiency

Leaves may turn pale or yellow, or growth may slow. Different nutrient shortages create different patterns.

General prevention

Use suitable soil and follow balanced fertiliser instructions. Do not add fertiliser without checking the plant's needs.
