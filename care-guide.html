Plant Care Guide

A few simple habits can help you notice problems early and keep plants in good condition.

1. Water thoughtfully

Check the soil before watering. Different plants need different amounts, and too much water can be harmful.

2. Provide suitable light

Place each plant where it receives the kind of light it needs. Check whether it prefers direct sun, bright indirect light or shade.

3. Check leaves regularly

Look at both sides of leaves for unusual spots, colour changes, holes or insects.

4. Keep tools and pots clean

Clean containers and gardening tools help reduce the spread of some plant problems.

5. Give plants enough space

Good spacing improves airflow and gives roots and leaves room to grow.

6. Be careful with fertilisers

Use a product suitable for the plant and follow its label. More fertiliser does not automatically mean healthier growth.
